# 📄 src/pages/ — Public Content Pages

This directory contains dedicated content pages rendered in the main application.

## 📁 Key Pages
- `AboutPage.tsx`: Company narrative, Italian production technology, and ISO quality pillars.
- `PortfolioPage.tsx`: Gallery of completed projects categorized by residential, hotel, commercial, and facade.
- `FaqPage.tsx`: Frequently Asked Questions accordion with real-time search filter.
- `ContactPage.tsx`: Contact Us form with Google Maps embed and showroom details.
- `StandardsPage.tsx`: Official ISO/INSO certificates and laboratory test results.
- `PrivacyPolicyPage.tsx`: Privacy policy legal skeleton.
- `TermsPage.tsx`: Terms of use legal skeleton.
- `NotFoundPage.tsx`: Custom 404 error page.
